01The promise BOND · The Four Promises of Care

What a caregiver is trying to keep true for a child.

Whatever changes around a child, the responsibility does not disappear. Families are still trying to keep them safe, connected, capable, and moving with direction. BOND gives every SageGap product and decision the same test: does this help the people around a child keep one of those promises?

The bond creates the responsibility. SageGap exists to help that responsibility travel with the child.

02The gapWhere the promises get harder to keep

The institutions can work, and the family can still lose the thread.

Schools, providers, therapists, evaluators, and families can each hold important pieces of a child’s story. The problem happens between them. Context gets separated, transitions erase history, and families are left to reconnect information that should have stayed connected in the first place.

When the thread breaks, care becomes harder. Protecting, connecting, preparing, and guiding a child all depend on the right context staying with the child through change.

A family crisis should not become an information crisis.

01

Records live in different places.

Families are expected to connect files, portals, people, and timelines that do not naturally connect themselves.

02

Transitions erase context.

A new teacher, school, district, provider, or caregiver can force the story to restart instead of continue.

03

Meetings move faster than understanding.

Dense documents and consequential decisions often arrive on institutional timelines, not family timelines.

04

Advocacy depends on memory.

Without a longitudinal view, prior supports, patterns, and promises can disappear exactly when they matter most.

03The connectionHow SageGap responds

Keep the people around a child connected.

SageGap helps relevant context travel between families, teachers, schools, providers, and supports. Think of it as connective tissue: it does not replace the people around the child. It helps them begin with shared context instead of repeatedly starting from zero.

The goal is continuity, not more complexity. The people may change. The child’s story should not have to restart.

07The trustWhat never changes

The family remains the steward.

Continuity only matters if the family remains in control of the story being carried forward. Technology can help make that story clearer. It should not quietly take ownership of it.

Intelligent systems may organize, compare, or suggest. They should not silently decide what is true about a child.

✓Human confirmation

Suggested information does not become canonical simply because software found it.

✓Source fidelity

The record preserves where information came from so context and provenance can be checked.

✓Permission based sharing

Access should be intentional, limited, understandable, and revocable.

✓Family continuity

The family keeps the thread even when institutions, staff, locations, or circumstances change.
