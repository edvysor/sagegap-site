How SageGap works

Keep the story intact. Make the next transition easier.

The landing page explains why continuity matters. This page shows how context comes together, how its source stays visible, how technology can reduce the sorting work without taking over, and how the family controls what moves forward.

The goal is not to give everyone more data. It is to help the right person begin with the right context.

The SageGap flow

Six steps. One continuing story.

This is the operating model SageGap is being built around. Each step protects continuity while keeping the family in control of what becomes trusted and what gets shared.

Canonical Egg01 · Bring context together.
The Family Record Bring the child’s context into one trusted view.
Bring the pieces into view.Different records can remain different records while becoming easier to understand together.
Family control stays constant through every step.
The highlighted step controls the Family Record visual.
1

Bring context together.

Learning records, evaluations, IEPs, goals, family notes, health or developmental supports, services, and important people can be viewed around one child instead of across disconnected places.

LearningWellbeingPeopleHistory
2

Preserve source fidelity.

SageGap is designed to keep the original source and its context traceable. A summary, comparison, or family note should not silently replace the record it came from.

Original sourceTraceableNo silent overwrite
3

Organize the Family Record.

The Canonical Egg gives the story a durable center: learning, wellbeing, the child’s circle, permissions, and longitudinal history remain connected instead of being reduced to one snapshot.

LearningWellbeingCircleContinuity
4

Let technology reduce the sorting work.

SageGap can help organize, compare, summarize, surface patterns, and suggest questions. It does not get to quietly declare truth, change the original source, or make the family’s decisions.

OrganizeCompareSummarizeSuggest
5

Family confirmation makes it canonical.

SageGap can suggest where information belongs or how pieces connect. The family confirms what becomes part of the trusted longitudinal record and controls the permissions around it.

Family confirmedPermissionedRevocable access
6

Make the next transition easier.

When a teacher, school, provider, program, or life circumstance changes, the family can share the context that matters for that moment. The next person can begin with understanding instead of starting from zero.

Relevant contextPurpose-limitedContinuity

The technology boundary

Helpful technology. Human authority.

When SageGap uses automated or AI-assisted tools, their role is limited: reduce sorting work and create clarity while original sources, permissions, and important decisions stay with people.

Technology may help

Reduce the work of making sense of the story.

✓
